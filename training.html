Training & FAQ

Welcome to our training and FAQ section! Here, you'll find information about our training programs, schedules, and answers to frequently asked questions.

Our training programs are designed for athletes of all skill levels, from beginners to advanced boxers. We offer a variety of classes and personal training sessions to help you achieve your fitness and boxing goals.

If you have any questions or need further information, please don't hesitate to contact us. Our team is here to assist you and provide the support you need on your boxing journey.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q; What should I wear to training?

A: We recommend wearing comfortable athletic clothing and boxing shoes. Bring a water bottle and towel for your convenience.

Q: Do I need prior boxing experience to join?

A: No prior experience is necessary! Our training programs cater to all skill levels, and our coaches will guide you through the fundamentals of boxing.

Q: How often should I train?

A: We recommend training at least 3-4 times per week for optimal results. However, the frequency of your training sessions will depend on your fitness goals and availability.
