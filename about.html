About Combox boxing

Founded in 2013, Combox boxing was created to bring discipline and technical perfection of sweet science boxing to athletes of every skill level. Our facility serves as a home for competitive fighters, fitness and beginers looking to push their physical bounderies and build unbreakable mental toughness.

We believe boxing is more than just a sport it is a transformative that instills confidence, focus and resilience.
